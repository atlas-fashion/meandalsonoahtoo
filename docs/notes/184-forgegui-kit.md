## 184. The last Tripo pieces remade with ForgeGUI (2026-11-14)

Noah asked for the rest of the dungeon remodel (note 183 did the corridor stone
in Blender) to be made with the ForgeGUI connector, not Blender, looking
realistic and atmospheric. Eight pieces were still Tripo; each was generated by
ForgeGUI (`generation_model_3d`, provider Meshy, PBR: albedo, metal-roughness
and normal maps at 2048) and fitted onto the Tripo piece's name, frame and size,
so nothing that places them moved.

**What and where.**
- Sources as they came back: `models/source/forge/*.glb` (door_leaf, jail_gate,
  prison_frieze, wall_loculi, morgue_slab, manhole_cover, prison_dome,
  torch_mount). The jail gate was generated twice: the first leaf's curved top
  fell towards the lock side, so a pair made a dip, not an arch; the prompt now
  says the top rises from the hinge to the lock stile.
- `tools/build_forge_kit.py` (Blender, as a fitting tool only) fits each to the
  Tripo box measured off `models/brick/brick_kit.glb` (the boxes are in its
  `PIECES` table, in Godot axes), decimates it, smooths by angle (50 degrees),
  shrinks the maps and writes `models/forge/forge_kit.glb` with JPEG maps, which
  Godot extracts next to it (`forge_kit_forge_<piece>_<map>.jpg`).
  - `door_leaf`: x and y to the Tripo leaf, 0.2 m of oak centred at z -0.58
    (the Tripo cut was 0.84 m thick). 2,500 triangles, 2048 maps.
  - `jail_gate_l`: x 0..1.64 from the hinge, 4.74 tall; `jail_gate_r` is its
    mirror (faces reversed), sharing the material. 4,000 each, 1024.
  - `prison_frieze` (6,000, 2048), `wall_loculi` (9,000, 2048, front at z 0),
    `morgue_slab` (turned 90 degrees so it is long along z; 6,000, 1024),
    `manhole_cover` (3,000, 1024): stretched to the box on every axis.
  - `prison_dome`: to the unit box, its floor faces cut away and every face
    reversed, since it is only ever seen from inside (as the Tripo dome was).
    13,600 triangles, 2048.
  - `torch_mount` (`fit_torch`): 1.44 m tall, its top at the origin; moved out
    whole so the head's centre stands 0.24 m off the wall (torch.gd
    `KIT_HEAD_OUT`, where the flame goes), and only the bracket arm (below the
    head, behind the shaft) stretched, 1.42x, so its plate still meets the
    wall at z 0.02. 3,000 triangles, 1024. Its wall plate is on the source's
    Blender +Y: no turn needed (a first fit turned it 90 degrees and hung the
    plate off the torch's side).
- `scripts/brick_level.gd` `_load_forge_kit()` runs after `_load_dungeon_kit()`
  and wins by name over both kits; `is_forge(piece)`; `standalone()` hands its
  meshes out as they are. Stone pieces (`FORGE_STONE`) get metal 0 and the
  albedo leaned cool (`FORGE_STONE_CAST`, the Blender stone's cast); no material
  takes vertex colour. `-- --no-forge` (or `--tripo-kit`) leaves the kit out.
- The exits' door (`door`, a MultiMesh piece in gate cells) has no generation
  of its own: `_forge_exit_door()` builds it from the Blender kit's `door_arch`
  with the ForgeGUI leaf in it, the arch brought out 0.437 m (its front onto the
  Tripo door's z 0.47) and the leaf LEAF_FIT larger at the arch's origin, as
  `module_level._build_brick_door` hangs it. `door_frame` is not remade: it is
  only the fallback when no `door_arch` exists.
- `scripts/manhole.gd`: the cover keeps its own cast iron when it is the
  ForgeGUI piece (it used to be given `_cover_material()` over the top).

**Measured** (RTX 3060, 3440x1440, `--bench`, runs alternated old / new /
old / new, old = `-- --no-forge`). The triangle budgets above are the Tripo
pieces' own; a first build at 8,000 for the door leaf and 9-12,000 for the
stone, with the maps imported lossless, read the same fps but +290 MB video
memory and +1.6-2.2 ms GPU in the chest room.

```
                 old run 1   old run 3   new run 4
chest room gpu    21.46 ms    20.82 ms    22.85 ms   (cpu 21.9 / 21.6 / 22.9)
surgery gpu       20.49       20.33       20.91
spawn corridor    11.14       10.81       10.83
OVERALL fps       56.4        57.5        55.0
video memory    1769.8 MB   1769.8 MB   1790.2 MB
```

- The chest room's GPU is still ~1.5 ms up (the prison: its dome, frieze,
  gates and torches now shaded as full PBR, metal and specular, where the Tripo
  pieces had specular off), but that station is CPU bound (cpu ms about equal to
  its frame), so its fps did not move. Overall fps 55.0 against 56.4 / 57.5 is
  within this machine's run-to-run drift. Video memory +20 MB.
- New run 2 (67.3 fps, the chest room at 108 fps, 1,407 MB) was an outlier;
  it is left out.
- The torch at 1,200 triangles shattered: the collapse decimation tore the thin
  shaft into shards. It is back at 3,000. Check thin pieces after lowering a
  budget. (The second runs above were measured with it at 1,200; at 3,000 each
  torch in view draws 1,800 more triangles, shadows off, which the first runs
  -- 3,000 too -- showed no fps for.)

**Traps.**
- ForgeGUI's 3D generation is a paid feature: on the free plan every call
  returns `paid_generation_required`. Generations then came back in 1-2 min.
- The glb's extracted maps import lossless (`compress/mode=0`) under a headless
  `--import`; set them VRAM compressed (`compress/mode=2`, normal maps
  `compress/normal_map=1`, `detect_3d/compress_to=0`) as the Blender kit's are.
- Meshy models come in normalised to about 1.9 m on their longest side, facing
  Blender -Y, so the front is already the Godot front.
